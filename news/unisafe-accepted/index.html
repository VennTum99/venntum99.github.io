UniSAFE accepted to NeurIPS 2026 AI4GOOD Workshop (Oral)

Our benchmark for safety evaluation of unified multimodal models was accepted for an oral presentation.

UniSAFE: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Safety Evaluation of Unified Multimodal Models was accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Trustworthy AI for Good (AI4GOOD) for an oral presentation. I am a co-first author with Segyu Lee and Hojung Jung.

The preprint and code are available. See the publication entry for the full author list and dataset access link.
