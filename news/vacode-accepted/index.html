VACoDe accepted to ICML 2024 TiFA Workshop

Our work on visual augmented contrastive decoding was accepted to the Trustworthy Multi-modal Foundation Models and AI Agents workshop.

VACoDe: Visual Augmented Contrastive Decoding was accepted to the ICML 2024 Workshop on Trustworthy Multi-modal Foundation Models and AI Agents (TiFA) on June 19, 2024. I am a co-first author with Sihyeon Kim.

The authors are Sihyeon Kim*, Boryeong Cho*, Sangmin Bae, Sumyeong Ahn†, and Se-Young Yun†. * denotes equal contribution; † denotes co-corresponding authors.
