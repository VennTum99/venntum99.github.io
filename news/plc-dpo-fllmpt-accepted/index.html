PLC-DPO accepted to NeurIPS 2026 FLLMPT Workshop

First-author work on noisy and ambiguous preference optimization, with Sumyeong Ahn and Se-Young Yun.

PLC-DPO: Posterior Label Correction in Noisy and Ambiguous Preference Optimization has been accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Foundations of LLM Post-Training in Changing Environments (FLLMPT). I am the first author, with Sumyeong Ahn and Se-Young Yun as co-corresponding authors.

The preprint and code are available. See the FLLMPT workshop entry and the separate Findings of EMNLP 2026 entry.
