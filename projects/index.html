Selected work

Weather foundation model

Yeobi

Role: Student Project Lead · Faculty Lead: Prof. Se-Young Yun

Jan 2026–present

Collaborators: National Institute of Meteorological Sciences (NIMS)

Lead development of a weather foundation model for global and regional forecasting, using HEALPix grids, adaptive spatiotemporal resolution, and variable attention.

Train models on multi-node GPU clusters and build meteorological data pipelines handling hundreds of TB.

Regional forecasting

AlphaRegion: High-Resolution Forecasting for East Asia

Role: Student Project Lead · Faculty Lead: Prof. Juho Lee

2025–present

Collaborators: NIMS and Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA)

Lead AlphaRegion development for high-resolution forecasting over East Asia.

Use AlphaCast global forecasts to increase both spatial and temporal resolution.

Precipitation forecasting

AlphaWeather

Role: Student Researcher · Faculty Lead: Prof. Se-Young Yun

Sep 2022–2025

Collaborators: NIMS

Managed training and evaluation of numerical weather prediction-based forecasting models and developed architectures combining multiple forecast inputs.

Contributed to AlphaRain, an AI-based precipitation forecasting model under consideration for operational use by the Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA).
